Find Your Level

Southbay Hoops divisions are split into three tiers so every team plays at the right pace and competition level.

Division A

Competitive Rec

For experienced players who played organized ball (high school, college, club) and want a faster pace, real defense, and competitive games each week.

Division B

Mid-Level Rec

For players with solid fundamentals looking for a fun, moderately competitive run — a step down in intensity from Division A, but still real basketball.

Division C

Beginner-Friendly Rec

For newer or casual players who want to learn, stay active, and enjoy the game in a relaxed, low-pressure setting.

Not sure which tier fits your team? Reach out and we'll help you find the right fit.
